How to fight

👆Move
Put your thumb anywhere on the LEFT side and drag.
⚔️Attack
Tap fast for a combo. Uses green stamina.
💨Roll
While rolling you can't get hurt. Roll right when you see the ✦ sparkle!
🧪Potion
Heals you, but it's slow. Drink when the monster is far away.
🟥Red = danger
Red shapes show where an attack will land. Get out — or roll through!
⭐Stagger
Fill the yellow bar to make the monster dizzy. Then hit it HARD!

On a computer: move with WASD or arrows · attack J or left-click · roll Space, Shift or right-click · potion E · spirit Q · pause Esc. In menus, Enter = fight / try again.
